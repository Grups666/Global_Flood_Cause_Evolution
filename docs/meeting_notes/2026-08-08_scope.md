# Meeting Scope: 2026-08-08

## Included topic

The project asks whether the causes of large river floods changed systematically
from roughly 1970 to 2020 across global catchments.

The proposed design focuses on rain-dominated catchments and excludes
snow-related floods from the initial analysis. Flood-producing rainfall should
be described as either short-duration/intensity-dominated or
long-duration/volume-dominated. Antecedent wetness should use satellite soil
moisture, with 1-, 3-, 7-, and 30-day windows tested. The intended output is a
global map showing where flood causes changed and the direction of change.

The meeting proposed studying the most extreme events, described orally as the
annual `top 5%`, and recommended reviewing Tarasova's event-typology work and
Manuela Brunner's work on extreme-event identification before fixing the exact
selection rule.
